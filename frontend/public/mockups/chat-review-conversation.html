Design notes for you to react to
  • Transcript below the reply box, as you asked — reads like Twitter, newest reply at the top of the thread.
  • Thread is a scrollable panel capped at ~260px so the yellow/green boxes stay visible without scrolling.
  • AI only replies when it needs to. If your first message is unambiguous (State A), it just updates the green box and drops a short "got it — here's what I did" line. No question, no confirm prompt.
  • When it does need to ask (State B), the green box goes into a pulsing "waiting on your reply" state so you know it hasn't committed yet.
  • Yellow and green boxes are still fully clickable at every state — you can override contact or book without ever chatting further.
  • Quick-reply chips (State B) let you resolve most follow-ups in one tap.
  • AI references the boxes conversationally ("see above", "I flagged PSG as the real counterparty") instead of by color.
