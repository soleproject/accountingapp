What v2 changes (4 things)
  1. Reply box loses the helper line above it. "Nothing posts until you confirm" is already implied by the green button below.
  2. Thread drops its container. No panel border, no "Conversation · 2 turns / Collapse" header. Just messages, with a tiny "AI" avatar so it still reads as a chat.
  3. Yellow heads-up collapses to a one-line strip with inline "Change contact" / "Keep as-is" buttons. Same info, ~⅓ the vertical space.
  4. Green box hides the form. Shows just the account summary + a big "Create & book" button. "Edit details" reveals the type/subtype/code fields on demand. Since the AI already summarized the intent in the transcript, we don't need to repeat "You said these are loans…"
Net result: same functionality, ~40% less vertical scroll, the primary action (Create & book) is almost always visible without scrolling.
