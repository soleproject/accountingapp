The 30-second pitch

To a business owner
“You know how you end up with a shoebox of receipts and a panic in March? SmartBooks connects to your bank, reads your receipts, and asks you maybe six questions a week on your phone. The books close themselves. It's $38–$149 a month and your accountant can log in free. Want me to send you my link? You get 14 days free instead of 7.”
To an accountant / bookkeeper
“What do you pay QuickBooks per client? … Right. SmartBooks is $349 flat for the firm and $15 a client, white-label included. The AI does the first pass and your clients answer questions in a 2-minute check-in instead of ignoring your emails. Give me 20 minutes to show you a sandbox firm.”
Who to talk to first
Highest-converting first 10 conversations
1 · People who've complained about their books

"My bookkeeper is always behind", "I hate QuickBooks", "I do it in a spreadsheet". They're pre-sold.

2 · Your own accountant

One firm = 10–50 clients downstream. Pro deals pay $69.80/mo each to you. Use the firm link.

3 · Trades & services under 10 staff

Plumbers, salons, landscapers, tutors — bank-feed heavy, receipt heavy, no finance person.
