## Frequently Asked

**Q: What if my rep signs a bad-fit CPA who churns in month 2?**
A: 6-month vesting clawback. That month's commission is reversed for both you and the rep. Encourages vetting.

**Q: What if I want to sell deals directly, not just recruit?**
A: You can. Register as both a rep AND a recruiter. Earn 20% on your own closes + 10% on your reps' closes.

**Q: Can my reps sell to their existing network (not CPAs)?**
A: The wholesale program is CPA-only. But we also have Scarlett Books (direct SMB) and SmartBooks Free (freemium) — separate compensation structures we can layer in later.

**Q: What territories are protected?**
A: Currently no hard territories — first-to-register-the-lead wins. But high-performing recruiters can request exclusive verticals (e.g., "all CPAs specializing in restaurant clients in Texas").

**Q: What happens if I stop actively recruiting?**
A: Your existing downline residuals continue as long as your reps stay active (defined as at least 1 close in trailing 90 days). If your reps go dormant, that residual pauses until they restart or you replace them.
