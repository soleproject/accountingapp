## Objection Handling — The Big 5

### 1. "We're already on QuickBooks — migration would be painful."
Our onboarding team handles migration. We import their entire QBO history for every client (chart of accounts, transactions, invoices, bills, contacts) in 24-48 hours. Zero data loss. You keep the same GL codes. Most firms are live within a week.

### 2. "How is this different from Xero?"
Xero replaces QBO. We replace QBO + your CRM + your project tracker + your team chat. Xero has a Partner Edition with basic project tracking; we have a full Business Operating System.

### 3. "What about integrations?"
We integrate with the tools you probably keep: Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, DocuSign, Plaid banks, Stripe for merchant history, IRS e-file services. We replace the tools you should drop: HubSpot, Monday, Asana, ClickUp.

### 4. "Pricing seems complicated."
It's actually simpler than QBO. One base fee + one per-client fee = your total bill. QBO has 4 tiers, each with add-ons, per-user fees, per-integration fees, and payments-processing markups.

### 5. "How do I know you'll be around in 5 years?"
Fair question. Two answers: (1) The founder is [background/proof], (2) The unit economics are strong — profitable per-customer margin — this isn't a burn-and-hope-for-Series-B play. And if we ever wound down, we'd export every client's data to QBO or Xero in a clean, complete format — that's a contractual commitment.
