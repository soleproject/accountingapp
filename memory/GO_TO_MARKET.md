## PART 1 — SERVICE OUTLINE

### The Product

**CypherPro** is an all-in-one Business Operating System for CPAs, bookkeepers, and their small business clients. One login replaces 5+ tools.

**What's inside (single login, unified data):**
- **Accounting** — Full double-entry books with GAAP/QBO parity: invoices, bills, payments, bank feeds, journal entries, financial statements, sales tax, multi-currency
- **CRM** — Contacts, deals, pipelines, activities, email sync — integrated with accounting so a "closed deal" becomes an invoice with one click
- **Projects** — Time tracking, budgets, project P&L, milestone billing — knows exactly what each engagement costs
- **Teams** — Internal messaging, task management, document sharing — replaces Slack + Asana for small teams
- **AI Copilot** — Voice actions, transaction auto-categorization, meeting recap parsing, "ask about my data" chat, CoA suggestions, KPI generation
- **Client Portal** — Branded portal where clients see invoices, upload receipts, sign engagement letters, message the CPA
- **Merchant Services** — Integrated card + ACH payments (SmartBooks Pay) with automatic reconciliation

### The Differentiators (vs QuickBooks / Xero / FreshBooks)

1. **Bundled** — Accounting + CRM + Projects + Teams for one price. QBO makes you pay $30/mo add-ons or integrate 5 tools.
2. **AI-native** — GPT-4o-mini + Claude Sonnet baked into every workflow. QBO's AI is bolt-on and limited to categorization.
3. **CPA-first architecture** — Firm dashboard, multi-client switcher, wholesale pricing tiers, white-label client portal. QBO ProAdvisor is retrofitted; ours is built for it.
4. **Payment margin sharing** — Optionally share your merchant services override with the CPA firm (0.10-0.20% rebate). QBO Payments keeps 100% of the take rate.
5. **Voice-first UX** — Meeting recap → tasks + emails + follow-ups in 30 seconds. Nobody else has this.

## PART 2 — PRICING STRUCTURE (CypherPro Wholesale)

### Tiered Base Fee + Per-Client Fee

| Firm Size | Base License | Per-Client | Effective Cost/Client (at midpoint) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Starter** (1-10 clients) | $99/mo | $15/client | $34.80 |
| **Growth** (11-30 clients) | $249/mo | $12/client | $21.96 |
| **Scale** (31-75 clients) | $349/mo | $10/client | $16.98 |
| **Enterprise** (76+ clients) | $499/mo | $8/client | Custom |

**Every tier includes unlimited admin users, full CRM/Projects/Teams, unlimited invoices, white-label client portal, and API access.**

### The CPA's Math

Example: **Growth-tier firm with 25 clients**
- Firm pays CypherPro: $249 + (25 × $12) = **$549/mo**
- CPA's cost per client: **~$22/mo**
- CPA charges each client: **$40-60/mo** (bundled into bookkeeping fee OR separate SaaS line)
- CPA's monthly profit margin on the platform line: **$450-950/mo**
- Plus: CPA's own productivity gain from AI + integrated tools = 30-50% more clients per staff member

### Payment Override (Separate Revenue Stream)

Every end-client processing cards through SmartBooks Pay:
- **CypherPro keeps: 0.40%** of card volume (via ISO/ISV rev share)
- **CPA optionally receives: 0.10%** rebate as a channel bonus (creates alignment)
- End client pays: standard 2.9% + $0.30 (no different than Stripe direct)

## PART 3 — COMMISSION STRUCTURE

### Two-Tier Rev Share (Percentage of MRR — Cleaner)

| Role | Payout | Recurring | Vesting |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Sales Rep** | 20% of MRR | Forever, while account active | 6-month clawback for churn |
| **Recruiter** | 10% of MRR | While their rep is active (deal in trailing 90 days) | Same 6-month clawback |
| **CypherPro** | 70% of MRR | | |

**Legal note:** Two-level only. No downline layers below the recruiter.

### Bonuses Layered On Top

| Trigger | Rep Bonus | Recruiter Bonus |
|---|---:|---:|
| Standard close (founder attended demo) | Base commission | Base 10% |
| **Unassisted close** (rep ran full cycle solo) | **+$300 one-time** (90-day vesting) | +$50 one-time |
| Rep hits 10 unassisted closes | — | +$100 one-time |
| Enterprise-tier firm signed (76+ clients) | +$500 one-time | +$250 one-time |

### Example Earnings

**Rep closing 3 firms/month (blended $417 MRR average):**
- Month 1: $375 recurring + up to $900 in unassisted bonuses = **$1,275**
- Month 12: $4,500/mo recurring (36 firms)
- Month 24: ~$7,600/mo recurring (assuming 85% retention)

**Recruiter with 5 active reps closing 3 firms/mo each:**
- Month 6: ~$3,750/mo passive
- Month 12: ~$7,500/mo passive
- Month 24: ~$12,500/mo passive

### Certification (Free)

All reps and recruiters must complete a free 2-hour certification before earning commissions:
- Product demo mastery (30 min)
- CPA sales pitch (30 min)
- Objection handling (30 min)
- Compliance / brand voice (30 min)
- Final: run a mock demo, pass with 80%+ score

Filter is effort, not money. Anyone hungry can qualify.
