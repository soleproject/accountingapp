### "We just renewed with QuickBooks."
Great — start migration planning now for your renewal date. Most firms migrate in stages, starting with new clients + high-value clients, then rolling the rest over 6-12 months. No pressure to move overnight.

### "My staff is used to QuickBooks."
CypherPro's accounting UI is intentionally similar to QBO (same GL structure, similar workflows). Staff typically ramp in 1-2 weeks. And the AI copilot actually reduces training time because it handles the "how do I do X?" questions.

### "What about integrations to [X]?"

Full export in QBO-compatible format (transactions, GL, invoices, bills, contacts) available in-app anytime. No hostage-taking.
