**Where we win in UK:** as strong on UK GAAP as Xero, better AI, cheaper per-client above ~15 clients.

## 9. FAQ (for landing page)

**Q: Can my clients cancel and take their data with them?**
A: Yes. Full CSV export. QuickBooks Online export. IIF for QuickBooks Desktop.

**Q: What happens if I cancel Practice Partner?**
A: Your firm keeps read-only access for 90 days. Clients can migrate to a self-serve plan or a competitor.

**Q: Is the white-label included in the $349?**
A: Custom colours + logo yes. Custom domain (yourfirm.com portal) is included on annual plans, or +$100/mo on monthly.

**Q: What if I have 500 clients?**
A: Above 250 clients we shift to enterprise pricing — lower per-seat rate + dedicated CSM. Reach out.

**Q: Do you support UK VAT / FRS 102?**
A: Yes. Full UK + US support. Companies House filings + HMRC-approved bookkeeping standards.

**Q: Can I resell to my clients at any price I want?**
A: Yes. You set client-facing pricing. We never see or bill your clients.

**Q: How do you handle client data privacy?**
A: SOC 2 Type II (in progress), UK GDPR compliant, client data isolated per firm. Full audit log.

**Q: Who owns the client relationship?**
A: You do. 100%. We're infrastructure — your clients never see us.
